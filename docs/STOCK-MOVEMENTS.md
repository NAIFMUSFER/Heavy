# Stock movement history

Status: under validation; not yet deployed. The read-only ledger exposes actual stock movements, canonical stock names and units, lot and document references, actor names, and optional cost entries. No historical balances, initial receipts, identities or costs are synthesized. Zero on-hand reservation events with no cost entry are distinct from physical movements with unknown or missing historical cost evidence.

`GET /api/ops/movements` permits admin/inventory/finance through the service-only `jana_stock_movement_history` RPC. Pages contain at most 50 rows, ordered by `(created_at,id)` descending, using paired `before_at`/`before_id` cursors. Filters accept exact `stock_id`, `lot_id`, `reason`, `reference` and millisecond `from_at` inclusive / `to_at` exclusive. Reference matches either the movement reference or the typed disposal document reference literally. Invalid, unknown or overlong filters are rejected in Edge and PostgreSQL. Cost and actor data are resolved server-side; account contact details and tokens are not returned.

The Arabic operations page provides movement type, exact document/order reference and inclusive calendar-date filtering in Saudi time (UTC+03), reset, and older-page loading. History reads do not change balances, reservations, cash, audits or inventory costs. New indexes support stable global/stock/lot pages. Physical-before/after amounts remain on the typed disposal record when recorded, rather than inventing a running balance from incomplete legacy history.

Tests cover real reservation/release and disposal cost joins, unknown cost versus absent cost entries, same-timestamp pagination with intervening inserts, time boundaries, literal references, role isolation, grants and actual browser filtering. Production verification remains pending.
